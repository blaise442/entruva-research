# Draft: data request for arXiv 2606.26028 (founder sends)

To: corresponding authors of arXiv 2606.26028
Subject: Data request: ERC-8004 ecosystem dataset for a preregistered failure-prediction study

Dear Dr. Xiong and co-authors,

I read "Can Trustless Agents Be Trusted? An Empirical Study of the ERC-8004
Decentralized AI Agent Ecosystem" (arXiv 2606.26028) with great interest,
in particular the dataset of on-chain identity and reputation events,
off-chain registration files, and x402 payment transactions crawled from
Ethereum, BNB Smart Chain, and Base through 2026-05-13.

We are running a 90-day preregistered study (preregistration frozen
2026-10-20, git tag prereg-v1) testing whether runtime behavioral telemetry
from AI agents predicts task failure out of sample well enough to serve as a
rating factor for agent liability insurance. ERC-8004 registry events are one
of our preregistered data sources, and your curated multi-chain dataset would
let us cross-check our own collection and extend coverage beyond Ethereum
mainnet and Base.

Would you be willing to share the dataset, or point us to a public release?
We would use it only for this study, cite the paper in any output, and share
our derived trace index and results with you before publication. Results will
be published whether positive or negative.

Thank you for considering this.

Best regards,
