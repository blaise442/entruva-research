Last week Base and BNB Smart Chain were two-day windows, because the free
endpoints serve anonymous clients only their last few days of history. The
founder approved a small spend on a paid endpoint, and this week both chains
were fetched in full: Base from its registry's deployment to 2026-11-02,
BNB the same.

The first attempt was wrong in a way worth describing. The provider accepts a
query over 50,000 blocks and answers it with a log list that is simply short,
with no error. Our count check noticed: BNB had 13 agent ids with no
registration event in our file, and a call to the contract showed those agents
exist. Querying the same blocks in small pieces returned 309 logs where the
wide query returned 293. The documented limit on that plan is 10,000 blocks,
and above it the answer is incomplete rather than refused. The collector now
asks for at most 10,000 blocks per call, both chains were fetched again, and an
integrity check passes on all three: registration ids run from 0 with no gap
and match the mint events one for one, every feedback sequence per agent and
client starts at 1 with no gap, and no log appears twice. The same check passes
on the Ethereum file from October.

The numbers. Base has 1,149,957 registered agents and 673,980 feedback events,
twenty times Ethereum on both counts. BNB has 401,341 agents and 30,144
feedback events. Across all three chains the median agent with feedback has
exactly one entry from one wallet, and the share of agents whose feedback
comes from a single giver is 0.92 on Ethereum, 0.91 on Base and 0.89 on BNB.
Base differs in scale rather than shape: one agent holds 390,890 of its
673,980 feedback entries, and 136,371 distinct wallets gave feedback, close to
one per agent. BNB has 143 feedback givers in total. The concentration we
described in week three is not an Ethereum artefact, and the cost of faking
on-chain reputation reported at Gate 4 reads across chains.

The research page's data section now carries the full-history table. What
remains is the weekly reports and the write-up due on day 90.
